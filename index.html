🎮 How to play (built for the 75" smartboard):
Each team gets 10 questions from the chosen chapter, with answer options shuffled independently for each team.
⚡ Answer FAST — as soon as YOUR team answers, you move straight to your NEXT question. You never wait for the other team!
✅ Correct → +1 mark and the rope pulls toward your side.
❌ Wrong or time runs out → no mark, no pull — but you still move on immediately.
🏆 Marks are tracked separately for each team, out of 10.
🏁 First team to pull the flag to their goal wins — or once both teams finish all 10, whoever's side the flag is on wins!
🔀 Every Start (and Play Again) draws a fresh shuffled question order — never the same as last time.
